# Clarify greater-than-c separation

**Learner**

but nothing relative to another?

**Tutor**

Yes—the **distance is between the two objects**. The distinction is *which frame measures its change*.

In Earth’s frame, Earth measures both ships’ positions at the same time and finds that their separation shrinks at \(1.6c\). If you ride on one ship and measure the other ship’s velocity in **your** frame, you get less than \(c\). So “the gap closes at \(1.6c\)” is relative to Earth’s frame, not a speed either ship measures for the other.
