# Remember complex conjugates

**Learner**

A=r here?

**Tutor**

No—**\(A\) and \(r\) mean different things here**:

- \(A\): the wave’s amplitude—its maximum displacement.
- \(r\): the distance from the source to the star.

In \(Ae^{i\theta}\), \(A\) is the complex number’s magnitude. The distance \(r\) appears **inside its phase**:
\[
Ae^{\,i2\pi(r/\lambda-\nu t)}.
\]
