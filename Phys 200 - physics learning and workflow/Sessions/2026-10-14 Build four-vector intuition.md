**Tutor**

For a particle with velocity components \(v_x,v_y,v_z\), its **four-velocity** is
\[
U=(\gamma c,\gamma v_x,\gamma v_y,\gamma v_z),
\qquad
\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}},
\]
where \(v\) is its speed in the frame you’re using.

*Generated*
