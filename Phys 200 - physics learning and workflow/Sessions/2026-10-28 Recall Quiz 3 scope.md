\[
\boxed{E_++E_-=U,\qquad E_+-E_-=-\frac{u}{c}U.}

**Learner**

How would you justify this question?

**Tutor**

The key justification is **momentum conservation**:

1. The parent is stationary, so total momentum is zero. The daughter and photon must leave with equal momentum magnitudes \(p\), in opposite directions.

2. The photon has energy \(pc\), so energy conservation gives
   \[
   4mc^2=E_d+pc.
   \]

3. The massive daughter satisfies
   \[
   E_d^2-p^2c^2=m^2c^4.
   \]
   Factoring and using step 2 gives
   \[
   E_d-pc=\frac{m^2c^4}{4mc^2}=\frac{mc^2}{4}.
   \]

4. Finally, for that same daughter,
   \[
   \frac vc=\frac{pc}{E_d}=\frac{15}{17}.
   \]

**Equal momentum magnitudes do not mean equal energies** when one product is massive and the other is a photon.
