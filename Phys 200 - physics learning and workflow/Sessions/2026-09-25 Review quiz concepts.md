Remind me gamma and reciprocal gamma in 2 term Taylor ?

**Tutor**

With \(\beta=v/c\), for **\(v\ll c\)**:

\[
\boxed{\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\beta^2}}\approx 1+\frac{\beta^2}{2}}
\]

\[
\boxed{\frac1\gamma=\sqrt{1-\beta^2}\approx 1-\frac{\beta^2}{2}}
\]

**Gamma: plus. Reciprocal gamma: minus. Both use \(\beta^2\), not \(\beta\).**
