**Tutor**

For **exactly two photons passing**, choose which 2 of the 4 pass:

\[
\binom42=\frac{4!}{2!\,2!}=6\text{ arrangements}.
\]

Each arrangement has probability \((1/2)^4=1/16\), so

\[
\boxed{P(\text{exactly 2 pass})=6\times\frac1{16}=\frac38}.
\]

In the area picture: **6 shaded cells out of 16**.
