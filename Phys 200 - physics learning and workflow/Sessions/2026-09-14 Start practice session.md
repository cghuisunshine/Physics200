Length contraction is L equals L naught times square root of one minus something. Time dilation, can you give two examples? Because I feel like this is a time dilation question.

**Tutor**

Yes—**length contraction and time dilation** are the two effects we’ve introduced.

### Length contraction

\[
L=L_0\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}
\]

\(L_0\) is an object’s length in its own rest frame. Observers who see it moving measure a shorter length **along its direction of motion**.

### Time dilation

\[
\Delta t=\frac{\Delta\tau}{\sqrt{1-\frac{v^2}{c^2}}}
\]

\(\Delta\tau\), called **proper time**, is the elapsed time on a clock present at both events. \(\Delta t\) is the longer elapsed time measured in a frame where that clock moves.

**Example 1: A spaceship clock**  
A ship travels at \(0.6c\). Between two ticks, its clock records 1 second. Earth’s frame measures:

\[
\Delta t=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=1.25\text{ seconds}.
\]

**Example 2: A particle’s lifetime**  
A particle lasts 8 nanoseconds in its own rest frame. If it moves through a lab at \(0.6c\), the lab measures:

\[
\Delta t=\frac{8}{\sqrt{1-0.6^2}}=10\text{ nanoseconds}.
\]

Yes, the pion question is a **time dilation** question. Its creation and decay are the two events; its own rest-frame lifetime is the proper time.
