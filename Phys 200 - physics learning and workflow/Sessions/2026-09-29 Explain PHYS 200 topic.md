# Explain PHYS 200 topic

**Learner**

oh two observers agree on their velocity

**Tutor**

**If you mean they agree on the magnitude of their relative velocity, yes.** If observer A measures B moving at \(+0.5c\), B measures A moving at \(-0.5c\).

But here, the key is that **the 1 light-year displacement and 2-year duration are measured in the same frame**, so their ratio gives \(0.5c\) in that frame. The traveller’s own frame has them at rest, with \(\sqrt3\) years passing on their clock.

**Learner**

positive delta s squared is spacelike?

**Tutor**

**Yes—with the sign convention you’re using:**
\[
\Delta s^2=\Delta x^2+\Delta y^2+\Delta z^2-c^2\Delta t^2.
\]

- **Positive:** spacelike.
- **Zero:** lightlike.
- **Negative:** timelike.

Some books reverse the sign convention, so always check the definition of \(\Delta s^2\).
