\[

Use:

- **Three terms only if requested or needed for the stated accuracy.** Nothing we’ve checked establishes a three-term requirement for Quiz 1.

Also, “two terms” counts the **\(1\)** as the first term. The next corrections would be \(+\frac38\beta^4\) for \(\gamma\) and \(-\frac18\beta^4\) for \(1/\gamma\).
