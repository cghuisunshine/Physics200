PART II: QUANTUM — OVERALL READING MAP

Main sources: Quantum Notes and selected OpenStax readings.
This map comes from the notes currently posted on Canvas (W1 2023, Version 2).
Quantum weekly assignments are not yet published; later course instructions
control the assigned sections, emphasis and timing.

Below, “Notes” means PHYS200__Notes_on_Quantum-4.pdf; “OpenStax” means
University Physics Volume 3 unless another book or volume is named.

1. Electromagnetic light: Notes §1.2; OpenStax Volume 2, Chapter 16 (review).
2. Light as a particle: Notes §§2.2–2.6; OpenStax §§3.1–3.2, 6.2–6.3.
   Photon detection: College Physics §31.2, only the photomultiplier paragraph
   and Figure 31.10. OpenStax §6.1 is further reading.
3. Polarization: Notes §3.2; OpenStax §1.7.
4. Quantum description of electrons: Notes §§4.2–4.3; OpenStax §§6.5–6.6.
5. Eigenstates and wavepackets: Notes §§5.2–5.6; OpenStax §6.5.
   OpenStax §7.1 is additional reading.
6. Uncertainty: Notes §6.2; OpenStax §7.2.
7. Schrödinger equation: Notes §5.6.2, then §§7.2–7.4; OpenStax §7.4.
   OpenStax §7.3 is strongly recommended before or after class.
8. Bound states and atomic spectra: Notes §§8.2–8.4; OpenStax §§7.4, 6.4, 8.1.
   Emphasis: discrete energies in §7.4, including Eq. 7.41; checking proposed
   Schrödinger solutions. OpenStax §7.5 is an additional example.
9. Tunnelling: Notes §9.1; OpenStax §7.6. Notes §§9.2–9.3 are optional.

Supplementary: Scherrer's Complex Numbers chapter, Readings from Knight,
potential-well material and PhET simulations. Notes' “Extra info” boxes are
nonessential extensions. Additional reading is not automatically assigned.
The module links alone do not assign entire OpenStax chapters.
