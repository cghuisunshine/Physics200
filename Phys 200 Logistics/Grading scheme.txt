PHYS 200 — Grading scheme

Pre-reading quizzes: 10%
Tutorial participation: 10%
Homework: 10%
Five long quizzes: 35% (7% each)
Final exam: 35%

Drop the lowest 2 marks in EACH of pre-reading, tutorials, and homework.
Long quizzes: lowest dropped, highest counted twice if all 5 are taken (or absence has a valid medical/emergency reason). Otherwise, maximum credit is 7% × quizzes completed.
Tutorials: full credit for a serious attempt. No lecture participation grade.
Pass requires BOTH overall grade >50% AND (long-quiz percentage + final percentage)/2 >45%.
